# KD Solution IT Automated Decision Review & Human Appeal Rights Policy

**Document Classification:** Mandatory Consumer Protection & Human Administrative Review Standard  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Governing Principles:** EU GDPR Article 22 (Automated Individual Decision-Making), California CPRA Automated Decision-Making Standards, and KD Solution IT Compliance Charter  
**Policy Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Absolute Prohibition on Solely Automated Adverse Determinations
KD Solution IT deploys advanced real-time heuristics, biometric verification models, and transaction velocity algorithms to screen merchant applications and transactions. However, platform governance guarantees that:

1. **Guaranteed Human Intervention:**
   - In accordance with GDPR Article 22 and consumer protection principles, **no merchant account is permanently terminated, barred, or placed on indefinite hold solely by automated algorithms**.
2. **Right to Meaningful Explanation:**
   - Any applicant or merchant subject to an adverse decision (e.g. automated KYC biometric failure, automated risk tier increase, or velocity transaction hold) has the right to receive a concise, plain-language explanation of the primary risk factors prompting the algorithmic determination.
3. **Presumption of Re-Examination:**
   - Every algorithmic signal is treated as a rebuttable presumption. Merchants maintain the affirmative legal right to present supplemental context, corrected filings, or disambiguating identity documents for human review.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Adverse Decision Appeal Workflow
Merchants may contest any adverse automated underwriting, risk scoring, or account limitation through the following formal procedure:

1. **Fourteen (14) Day Appeal Window:**
   - The merchant must submit a formal appeal through the public appeal portal (`/legal/appeal`) or via email within fourteen (14) calendar days of receiving the automated adverse decision notice. Appeals received after 14 calendar days are barred absent extraordinary circumstances.
2. **Required Information in Appeal Submission:**
   - Official merchant company legal name and corporate tax identification number.
   - Decision Reference Identifier (e.g. `DEC-2026-XXXXX`).
   - Detailed factual grounds for appeal explaining why the automated heuristic reached an inaccurate conclusion.
   - Supplemental corroborating evidence (e.g. updated Certificate of Good Standing, manufacturer supplier invoices, unexpired secondary photo identification, or audited financial statements).
3. **Automated Acknowledgment & Dossier Ingestion:**
   - Upon submission, the merchant receives an immutable cryptographic appeal receipt with a unique tracking identifier.

---

## 3. Compliance Review SLA & Chief Executive Officer Escalation
1. **Five (5) Business Day Determination SLA:**
   - The Compliance Department, under the supervision of the designated Anti-Money Laundering Compliance Officer (AMLCO), re-examines the entire underwriting dossier de novo (from the beginning) without algorithmic deference.
   - A formal written determination (Approval, Conditional Approval with Modified Terms, or Final Denial) is issued within five (5) business days of appeal submission.
2. **Executive Escalation Channel to CEO:**
   - If the merchant believes the Compliance Department's written determination was reached in error or misapplied governing regulations, the merchant may request an Executive Escalation.
   - The Chief Executive Officer, in consultation with outside regulatory counsel, conducts an executive review and issues a final, binding administrative determination.
