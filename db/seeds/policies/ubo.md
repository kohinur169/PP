# KD Solution IT Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO ≥ 25%) Identification Policy

**Document Classification:** Mandatory Regulatory Compliance Disclosure & Ownership Attestation Standard  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Governing Regulations:** FinCEN Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Final Rule (31 CFR § 1010.230), EU 5th and 6th Anti-Money Laundering Directives, and FATF Recommendation 24  
**Policy Version:** 1.0  

---

## 1. Regulatory Framework & Mandatory Scope
KD Solution IT enforces strict identification of Ultimate Beneficial Ownership across all corporate, LLC, partnership, and institutional sub-merchants. In full accordance with the United States Department of the Treasury Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) Customer Due Diligence rule and international Financial Action Task Force (FATF) standards, legal entity merchants must formally identify and verify all natural persons exercising significant economic ownership or executive control over the business entity.

---

## 2. The 25% Ownership Prong
1. **Identification Threshold:** Any natural person who, directly or indirectly through contractual agreements, holding companies, trusts, or intermediate corporate vehicles, owns twenty-five percent (25%) or more of the equity, shares, capital, or profit interests of the merchant legal entity is classified as an Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO).
2. **Mandatory Documentation for &ge; 25% Owners:**
   - Full legal name, date of birth, residential street address, and nationality.
   - Government-issued taxpayer identification number (e.g. Social Security Number or foreign national tax ID).
   - Unexpired government photo identification (Passport or National Identity Card).
   - Biometric selfie liveness challenge verified through anti-spoofing certified providers.
3. **Complex Ownership Structures:** For multi-tier corporate hierarchies, the merchant must submit an official capitalization table and certified organizational chart showing the exact chain of equity leading to the ultimate natural human beneficiaries.

---

## 3. The Executive Control Prong
In addition to all 25% or greater equity holders, every legal entity applicant must identify at least one (1) natural person who exercises significant executive control or managerial decision-making over the company:
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), or Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
- Managing Member, General Partner, or Executive Vice President.
- The designated control person must execute the formal digital attestation certifying the accuracy and completeness of all declared beneficial ownership information.

---

## 4. Prohibited Nominee & Bearer Share Arrangements
1. **Bearer Shares:** Entities issuing or utilizing bearer shares, unregistered equity instruments, or blind voting trusts are strictly ineligible for onboarding or gateway activation.
2. **Nominee Directors:** Nominee directors or non-beneficial straw owners deployed to obscure natural ownership are illegal and trigger immediate referral to regulatory authorities.

---

## 5. Ongoing Sanctions Screening & Periodic Re-Certification
1. **Continuous Watchlist Cross-Referencing:** Every declared UBO and control person is submitted to automated daily screening against OFAC SDN, UN, EU, and UK sanctions lists, as well as global Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) registries.
2. **Annual Re-Certification Mandate:** Merchants must re-certify their beneficial ownership structure annually or within thirty (30) days of any material equity transfer, ownership restructuring, or executive leadership change.
3. **Breach Consequences:** Failure to disclose a 25% or greater beneficial owner or submitting fraudulent ownership attestations results in immediate account suspension, gateway deactivation, and mandatory FinCEN Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing.
